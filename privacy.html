English

Hushcut processes video and audio on your device. The developer does not collect your videos, audio, identifiers, usage analytics or purchase history on a server. The app has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, cloud processing or AI service.

When you choose a video, iOS provides a temporary local copy. If the original is stored only in iCloud, Apple's picker may download it through your Apple account. Processing requires a locally available copy. We do not operate the iCloud transfer.

The app keeps your language and default editing settings locally. Temporary video copies are removed when you replace or clear the edit, or at the next app launch. Temporary data may remain until then after a crash. Videos you explicitly save to Photos remain in your library until you delete them. Files sent through Share are handled by the app or service you choose and its privacy terms.

Apple processes payments, refunds and purchase restoration. The app checks Apple-signed StoreKit transactions on your device to determine whether unlimited exports are unlocked. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple's own store and operating-system privacy practices apply separately.

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you and do not share them with anyone.

You can clear the current edit in the app, revoke Photos permissions in iOS Settings, or uninstall the app to remove its local data. Saved or shared copies must be deleted separately. For privacy questions, email 1375799606@qq.com.

動画選択時、iOSが一時的なローカルコピーを提供します。元の動画がiCloudにのみ存在する場合、Appleの選択画面がAppleアカウントを通じてダウンロードすることがあります。処理には端末内のコピーが必要です。この転送を開発者が運営することはありません。

メールでお問い合わせいただいた場合、メールアドレスと内容は返信のためにのみ使用し、第三者に提供しません。

編集のクリア、iOS設定での写真アクセス許可の取消、アプリの削除によりローカルデータを管理できます。保存・共有済みのコピーは別途削除してください。お問い合わせは 1375799606@qq.com までご連絡ください。
