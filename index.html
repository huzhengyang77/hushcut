FAQ

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple account you bought with.

What can I export for free?

Import, analysis and full preview are free. Original videos up to 60 seconds export for free; longer originals need the one-time Unlimited Exports purchase. No subscription, no watermark.

Why was a quiet word cut?

Detection uses volume, not speech recognition. Lower the silence threshold or raise the padding around speech, listen to that gap, or set it to Keep.

Does my video leave my phone?

No. Everything is processed on your device. See the privacy policy.

常见问题

怎么恢复购买？

在 App 的「设置」里点「恢复购买」，需要登录购买时用的同一个 Apple 账号。

哪些可以免费导出？

导入、分析和完整预览都免费。原视频不超过 60 秒可免费导出；更长的原视频需要一次买断「无限导出」。无订阅、无水印。

为什么有个轻声的字被剪掉了？

检测依据的是音量，不是语音识别。可以调低静音阈值、调高说话前后保留，试听那一段，或者把它切换为「保留」。

视频会上传吗？

不会，全部在手机上处理。详见隐私政策。
